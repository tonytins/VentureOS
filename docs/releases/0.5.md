# VentureOS 0.5, Cassiopeia

VentureOS 0.5 is about how the desktop looks: pictures and icons, a glossy menu bar with a clock, GNOME-style
windows and sidebars, GNOME's colors, and choices for the look, down to automatic dark mode. The last build is
0.5.506, tagged `v0.5.506`. The [changelog](../../changelog.md) lists each build's changes, from 0.5.100 to
0.5.506.

## Before you start

- VentureOS runs from memory. Files you create or change, your apps, your look, and your clock's offset last until
  you reboot or shut down.
- It's built and run under QEMU on x64. It builds for ARM64 too, but hasn't been booted there.
- 0.5 is the only supported release. Until 1.0, only the latest release is.
- The hardware clock is read as UTC. Set your offset from UTC once (see [Set the clock](#set-the-clock)), or the
  menu bar shows UTC.

## The desktop

- **Wallpaper.** The desktop shows Earthset, the Earth setting behind the Moon. Choose the sky instead in Settings.
- **Icons.** Apps show their icons in the dock and in their About window. An icon with a full background is rounded
  to a tile; one drawn as a shape keeps it, with a soft shadow.
- **The menu bar** has Tiger's gloss, VentureOS's icon in place of its name (it turns white while its menu is open),
  and the time on the right, such as `Tue 3:45 PM`.

## Windows

- **Header bars.** Title bars are flat and blend with the window, with a faint line under them. Toolbar items are
  flat too, shaded only under the pointer.
- **Zoom.** Resizable windows have a green third button: it fills the screen below the menu bar, and a second click
  puts the window back.
- **Rounded corners** on all four corners, and rounded shadows. Switch them to square in Settings.
- **Sidebars** mark the selected row with a rounded shade, and can split rows into groups: Files sets System apart
  from Home, User, and Apps. Files also shows how many items a folder holds, under its address.

## Settings

Settings has a sidebar with two panes, as GNOME Settings does.

| Pane | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Appearance | Light, Dark, or Auto, and when Auto is dark; the accent, from GNOME's nine or your own `#RRGGBB`; rounded or square window corners; and the wallpaper, from thumbnails |
| Clock | Your offset from UTC, typed after the "UTC" label, such as `-05:00` or `5:30` |

VentureOS takes GNOME's colors in light and dark: neutral windows, sidebars, and menus, with only the accent in color.
An accent you chose in 0.4 still works, shown as Custom.

## Set the clock

```sh
clock                      # the time, the date, and your offset from UTC
clock -05:00               # five hours behind UTC; -5, 5:30, and 0 work too
appearance auto            # dark from 7 PM until 7 AM, by the clock
appearance auto --from 20:00 --until 06:30
```

There's no daylight saving: change the offset when the clocks change.

## Write apps

[Writing apps](../writing-apps.md) covers each of these. 0.5 adds to what an app can use:

- `icon` in `Info.xml`: a PNG shown in the dock and About. Paint what should be clear in exactly `#00FF01`.
- Images in `.uix` files: `<Image path>` beside the document, shown by a `<Visual>` or a `<Button>`, and
  `window:setImage(name, path)` to change one while the app runs.
- In the title bar: `subtitle` on `<Window>` or `window:setSubtitle(text)`, and toolbar items with an `image`, drawn in
  the text's color, at either end with `placement="start"`.
- A sidebar's rows as tables in `window:setItems`, with an `icon` and `{ separator = true }` between groups.
- Scripts: `venture.time()` and `venture.setUtcOffset(offset)`, `venture.wallpapers()`, and the whole look from
  `venture.appearance()` and `venture.setAppearance{ ... }`.
- Arguments that start with a dash and a digit, such as `-05:00`, are values, not options.

## Known limits

- Toolbar items and sidebar rows show text until their icons are drawn; none ship yet.
- Wallpapers can carry a light and a dark picture, but none ships with them yet.
- Settings shows up to four wallpapers besides the sky.
- Auto follows a schedule you set, not the real sunset and sunrise.
- A zoomed window leaves a thin strip of wallpaper under the menu bar.
- Terminal's output can't be selected or copied, and Terminal's window can't be resized.
